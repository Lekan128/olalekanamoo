No. 02What carried over

Same instincts, new material

I changed fields, but I still work the way I was trained to.

  1. i.

    Stress analysis → load planning

    Work out where a structure breaks before you put weight on it. With software, that means profiling before optimising and knowing a system's limits before launch day.

    2,000+ concurrent uploads, flat memory

  2. ii.

    Tolerances → reliability

    Every part has an allowed margin of error. Every service has one too, and a migration has to stay inside it.

    300+ tenants moved, zero downtime

  3. iii.

    Cost analysis → sensible architecture

    The best design is the one that does the job without waste, whether that's steel on a sheet press or instances on AWS.

    Simple systems that are cheap to run

  4. iv.

    Diagnose, then repair

    The workshop taught me never to swap a part on a hunch. In production, that means isolating causes before shipping a fix.

    Memory incident traced to two causes
